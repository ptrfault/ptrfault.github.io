About

I started getting into reverse engineering because I hated not understanding things. I'd spend stupid amounts of time opening stuff in debuggers just to see what was happening.

That eventually turned into an interest in runtime behavior, software protection and understanding why systems are built the way they are.

What I like about security is that a lot of problems don't really have a perfect answer. Most of the time you're just trying to build something slightly better than whatever existed before.
